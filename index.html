01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

A curious kid
with a long list of “what ifs.”

Part people person, part experiment enthusiast. Always interested in what happens when an idea leaves the notes app and meets the real world.

I’m Trisha - an undergraduate at Mesa School of Business, currently in that very exciting stage of figuring things out by actually doing things. I’m interested in how businesses get built, why people love certain brands, what makes someone stop scrolling, and how a small idea turns into something real.

I have a slightly unreasonable appetite for trying things. I’ll sign up for something I’ve never done, talk to someone whose work I find interesting, or volunteer for the part that makes me a little nervous. I don’t need to be good at something before I start; I’d rather learn while doing it.

I love meeting new people and asking questions. I take opportunities seriously, but I don’t take myself too seriously. Right now, I’m exploring different industries, making mistakes, and getting a clearer sense of where I can do my best work.

02 / SUSTAINABILITY + OPERATIONS

KABAADAI.

EPR compliance × plastic traceability

I was involved in a Kabaadai project focused on Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) compliance, including work around a plastic traceability dashboard. It introduced me to a different side of business: how systems, data and accountability can come together to solve a real operational problem.

The problem space: plastic waste, compliance requirements and the challenge of keeping material movement traceable.

What I explored: how a dashboard can make traceability and compliance information easier to track and act on.
